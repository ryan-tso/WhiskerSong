Why WhiskerSong
  • 🐇

    Exotic animal expertise

    Rabbits and small exotics have unique needs that many general vets aren't trained to address. Our practice is focused on these little ones.

  • 🏠

    Less stress

    Car travel is a top stressor for small animals. Home visits reduce anxiety, making exams more accurate and recovery faster.

  • 📱

    Follow-up care

    Still have questions or concerns after the visit? Feel free to reach out. Ongoing support is a part of what we do!

  • 🔍

    Observed in their safe space

    A pet may hide pain or certain behaviours at a clinic due to anxiety and stress. Observing them at home increases the chance of picking up any subtle signs of illness.

  • ⏱️

    No waiting rooms

    No sitting in a waiting room with barking dogs or other fractious animals. Relax in your home until we arrive.

  • 🤝

    Care built on relationships

    We are focused on establishing relationships with our clients and their pets to produce the best outcomes for everyone.
