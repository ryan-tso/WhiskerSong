Cancellation Policy

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. This allows us to offer the time slot to another patient in need.

Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a cancellation fee of $50. We understand that unexpected situations arise — please reach out as soon as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Payment Policy

Payment is due at the time of the visit. We accept e-transfer, credit card, and cash.

For e-transfers, payment can be sent to billing@whiskersong.com. Payments through e-transfer or cash receive a 1.5% discount. Please note that we do not carry change for any cash payments.

If you have questions about pricing ahead of your visit, please don't hesitate to ask — we're happy to provide an estimate based on your pet's needs.
