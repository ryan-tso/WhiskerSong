Wellness Exams

Annual or bi-annual exams are important to catch signs of early disease. Catching teeth spurs early vs. late may mean the difference between a simple diet change vs. more serious complications like gut stasis.

  • Full physical exam
  • Preliminary dental assessment including molar spurs in rabbits and guinea pigs
    (*Note that a full oral exam requires sedation)
  • Husbandry guidance and other advice
💉
Vaccinations

Vaccinations are important to prevent serious and fatal diseases such as rabies, rabbit hemorrhagic disease, and ferret distemper. Routine parasite treatment is important to prevent flea infestations and insect-borne diseases such as Lyme.

  • We carry most vaccines for rabbits, ferrets, cats, and dogs
  • Vaccine certificates and certificates for travel
  • Parasite prevention discussion
⚠️ Parasites and the diseases we vaccinate against can affect indoor pets as well.
🔬
Diagnostics

Physical exams can only reveal so much. Often further diagnostics are required to investigate further. We can provide all tests offered by external labs.

  • Blood tests — CBC & full biochemistry panels
  • Urinalysis — kidney function, crystals, infections
  • Fecal tests for parasites & other infectious agents
  • Microscopy & cytology
  • Referral to a partner clinic if X-ray or ultrasound is indicated
🤒
Sick Pet Exams

Home visits avoids the compounding stress of transport when your pet is already sick.

  • Thorough and non-rushed assessment of your ill pet
  • GI stasis evaluation & treatment — a life-threatening emergency
  • On-the-spot treatment and referral if needed
  • Follow-up calls to check in after the visit
⚠️ Depending on the cause, the prognosis in gut stasis cases can drop dramatically by each hour. Contact us as soon as something seems wrong.
🩹
Minor Procedures

Many common interventions can be performed safely and gently at home. Recovery also begins somewhere comfortable and familiar.

  • Minor wound and abscess management
  • Subcutaneous fluid therapy
  • Nail trims, anal gland expression, and minor grooming
  • Incisor trimming
  • Bandaging and cast placements
  • Microchip implantation
Some procedures require sedation.
🕊️
Euthanasia & Farewell

You and your companion deserves a peaceful, unhurried farewell in the space they have always known. We are honoured to walk this path with you.

  • Fully in-home euthanasia
  • 3 or 4 step process, beginning with gentle sedation
  • No time pressure
  • Aftercare and cremation coordination & pet loss grief support
For giant breed dogs, please contact us to discuss as coordination and planning is required prior to the visit.
